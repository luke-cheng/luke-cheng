## Done but feel empty

I've been staring at the application's "Thank you for your application" page blankly, & feel empty.

Bc I recalled how I did a similar challenge for Ramp job app a few year back before the AI era.

This was my old approach before gen AI was widely adopted.

- Figure out it's base64 encoded 
- decode via online base64 decoder
- google to find out I'll need hashcat to do this job
- set up & spin up my Kali usb drive
- find a english dictionary online & turn it into .txt as the dictionary attack 
- read the Hashcat manual to find the correct command & prep the target files
- A few trail & errors finally execute the correct hashcat command on my usb Kali linux

There was curiosity, excitement, most importantly a sense of achievement. Now I just kind of ask AI everything. Follow's AI Run the exact command AI give. AI even tell me that there's a better dictionary file (rockyou.txt). I don't know how I should feel but there was definitely something there which no longer exist.
