## Do you remember how we write before gen AI?

I wish you remember those experience of writing essay at midnight, way before gen AI is a thing. The word documents quietly in front of you, staring blank at you. The cursor blinked lifeless, ticking like a clock. And you are frustrated, it's the third time you type in, then immediate delete a line. And the clock hit 1am, you decided, f it, and start dumping random thoughts directly into the docs, and words count slowly ticking up. After a while of typing, you stopped, and look back to the vomit you created, and start put them into formatted, structural paragraph. Because a decent articles starts with some good thoughts, 润色 with 起承转合.
