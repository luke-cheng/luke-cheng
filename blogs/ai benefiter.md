Before I was a software dev, I was a chemist, and before that, I was a college student who majored in chemistry. I was never a good researcher because a lack of interest in chemistry literature. But I'm damn good at searching. I found a lot of useful tools to make my college life easier. i.e. some matrix calculator for Physical chem, some obscure chemical energy calculator,propagation of uncertainty calculator, or just a simple tool to draw chemical structure. As I was dig it though, I found some were running, and some are not. Back then I would have no way fixing it, as I have no programming skills, the source code might as well like astrological tome for me. Even though they generously open source them on github. 

Looking back ot it, most of the time a tool is broken is because of a deprecate package. Fixing it don't really take much skill but just need knowledge on which replacement package should being use and some just tedious work to iron out the different parameter. Ai is extreme helpful on this type of task.
